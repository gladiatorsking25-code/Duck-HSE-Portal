Not legal advice. This is a draft starting point, written with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data ("PDPL") in mind. Have it reviewed by a UAE-licensed lawyer before commercial launch — the right approach can differ depending on whether your business is onshore, or licensed in a free zone such as DIFC or ADGM (which have their own, separate data protection regimes that can apply instead of or alongside the federal PDPL).
1. What personal data this app handles

Depending on how you use it, the App may store:

  • Names of assessors, issuers, approvers, verifiers, persons in charge, and contractors entered into assessments and permits;
  • Names of inspectors, operators and supervisors, and the equipment identifiers (asset, plate and serial numbers) entered into monthly equipment checklists;
  • Photographs of third-party certificates you attach to a checklist — inspection certificates, operator/rigger/banksman competency certificates and lifting-accessory certificates. These images can contain personal data (a named certificate holder, a photograph on a licence, an issuer's stamp). They are resized and stored on your own device in the browser's IndexedDB, alongside the certificate details (number, issuer, issue and expiry dates) held in the checklist record;
  • Email addresses you type into the checklist forwarding screen. These are used only to address that one message and are handed straight to your own email app — they are not stored by the App, and there is no address book or sending history;
  • Handwritten signatures captured on-screen for permit sign-off;
  • Free-text notes you choose to enter;
  • Sign-in identifiers used by the app's access gate (see the Terms of Use and the app's README for how that gate currently works).

It does not collect health data, financial/payment card data (payment for any paid subscription is handled entirely by Google Play, not by this app), precise device location, or advertising identifiers.

2. Where this data is stored today

In the current build, all of the data listed above is stored only in your device's browser storage (localStorage), scoped to the page you open the App from. Nothing is transmitted to any server — there is no backend in this build. That means:

  • The developer/operator cannot see, access, or process your data, because it never leaves your device;
  • Data does not sync between your devices automatically;
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app if wrapped for an app store, deletes this data permanently unless you have exported a backup (see the Fleet & Backup page).
3. If cloud sync / accounts are added later

A paid, multi-device version of this App would need a real backend (for example Firebase or Supabase) to sync data and verify subscriptions. If and when that happens, this notice will be updated to name the specific processor(s) used, where they host data, and the safeguards applied to any cross-border transfer, as required by PDPL Articles 20–24. Until this notice is updated to describe such a backend, no such transfer is taking place.

3a. Cross-border transfer, if and when cloud sync is switched on

The optional cloud-sync build uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as a data processor. Firebase does not currently offer a UAE data-region, so enabling cloud sync means personal data you enter is transferred to and stored outside the UAE — in whichever Google Cloud region the Firestore database is created in, which is chosen once and cannot be changed afterwards.

PDPL Articles 22 and 23 permit such a transfer where the destination has an adequate level of protection recognised by the UAE Data Office, or — failing that — where it is covered by an appropriate contractual safeguard or by your explicit consent after being informed of the risk. The safeguard relied on here is Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, together with the consent recorded through the in-app acceptance screen. If you do not want your data leaving the UAE, do not create a cloud account — the app is fully functional without one, and local-only data never leaves your device.

Before enabling cloud sync commercially, confirm with a UAE-licensed lawyer which Firestore region to choose and whether your specific customers (government or critical-infrastructure clients in particular) are subject to data-localisation requirements stricter than the general PDPL position.

3b. Payments and subscriptions

Any paid subscription is sold and processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The App never sees, receives, or stores your card number, bank details, or billing address. What the App may receive from Google is a purchase token and subscription status used solely to unlock paid features. Google's own privacy policy governs the payment itself.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where the App does process personal data (locally, as described above), the basis relied on is your consent, given when you accept these terms via the in-app consent screen, and/or the performance of the lift-planning and permit-to-work record-keeping function you are using the App for.

5. Your rights under PDPL

Subject to the exceptions PDPL provides, you have the right to: request access to personal data concerning you; request correction or erasure of it; restrict or object to its processing; request its transfer to another controller (data portability); and withdraw consent at any time. Because this build stores data only on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights directly — edit or delete any record from the History, Permits, or Fleet & Backup pages, or use "Erase all local data" on the Fleet & Backup page to remove everything at once.

6. Signatures

On-screen signatures are stored as images alongside the permit record, in the same local storage as everything else. They are used solely to represent sign-off on a permit-to-work document and are not analyzed, shared, or used for any other purpose by the App.

7. Children

This App is intended for use by adult workplace safety professionals and is not directed at children. It should not knowingly be used to collect personal data from children.

8. Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been processed in a way that is not consistent with PDPL, you may raise this with the developer/operator using the contact details in the app footer, and/or with the UAE Data Office, which administers PDPL, or the relevant free-zone data protection authority (DIFC or ADGM Commissioner of Data Protection) if applicable to the entity operating this App.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated as features change (particularly if cloud sync or a payment backend is introduced). The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Retention — how long data is kept

Local data is kept on your device until you delete it; nothing expires on its own, and the developer has no ability to delete it for you. If cloud sync is enabled, synced records are kept while the account is active and deleted within 30 days of an account-deletion request, except where a record must be retained to meet a legal obligation (for example accounting and VAT records relating to a paid subscription, which UAE tax law requires be kept for a set period). Retained records are kept only for that purpose and deleted at the end of the statutory period.

11. How to delete your data

Every deletion route — erasing all data on the device, uninstalling, and requesting deletion of a cloud account and its synced records — is set out on the Delete your data page, which is publicly reachable without signing in. That page is also the account-deletion URL submitted to Google Play.

12. Data controller and contact

The data controller is the developer/operator of the App, whose current contact details are shown in the app footer and on the About & legal page. Once the operating business is licensed in the UAE, the licensed entity name, trade licence number, and registered address will be published here and in the Google Play listing, as Google Play's developer-verification rules require.

13. Google Play Data Safety

The answers given in the Play Console Data Safety form mirror this notice: in the standard build, no data is collected or shared by the developer, all data stays on the device, and the user can request deletion. If cloud sync is enabled in a published build, that form will be updated the same day to declare the data types collected, that they are transmitted over HTTPS, and that deletion can be requested — Google checks the form against the app's actual behaviour, and a mismatch is a policy violation.
