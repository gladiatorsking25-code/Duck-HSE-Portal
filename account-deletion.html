The standard build has no server. If you never created a cloud account, deleting the app or clearing its data removes everything, and there is nothing held about you anywhere else.

Option 1 — Erase everything on this device now

This permanently deletes every assessment, permit, signature, note, saved preference, and the offline copy of the app from this device. It cannot be undone. Export a backup first from Fleet & Backup if you need the records.

Export a backup first
Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Duck HSE Portal → Storage → Clear storage, deletes all locally held data as well. In a desktop browser, clear site data for this site from the browser's privacy settings.

Your rights

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data you may also request access to, correction of, restriction of, or transfer of personal data concerning you, and withdraw consent at any time. See the Privacy Notice for the full description and for how to complain to the UAE Data Office.
