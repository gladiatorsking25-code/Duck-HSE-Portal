Safety scope — read this before relying on the app
This is a planning and record-keeping aid, not an authority. Checklists, permits, audit packs and assessments support competent people; they do not replace their judgement, your site's procedures, or the law.
  • Checklists and audit templates are starting points. Adapt them to your equipment, your site's risk assessments and your client's requirements.
  • Applicable regulations, such as the ADOSH-SF codes of practice and technical guidelines, take precedence over anything shown here.

Permits to work

  • The permit forms support your site's permit-to-work system; they do not replace it. Only people your site has authorised may issue, approve or close permits.
  • The precautions, gas-test limits and close-out checks are general examples based on the ADOSH-SF codes of practice named on each form. Check them against your site's procedures, and use the stricter figure where they differ.
  • Gas tests must be carried out by a competent person with a calibrated, bump-tested detector. The app only records the readings; it cannot check the instrument.
  • Permits to work should follow ADOSH-SF CoP 21.0 – Permit to Work Systems and any other applicable local regulations, which take precedence over anything shown here. CoP 21.0 asks for permit records to be kept for at least 1 year: close permits rather than deleting them, and keep exported backups.

Lifting operations

  • Every lift must be verified by a competent Appointed Person against the crane manufacturer's certified, current load chart before work begins.
  • Load-chart figures on file are transcribed from manufacturer specification sheets. Verify them against your paper copy before first use and whenever a crane is added.
  • Wind limits apply whichever is lower of the crane's rated limit and the ADOSH-SF site stop-work threshold. Your site's appointed person sets the final figure.
  • Exclusion-zone distances are a calculated starting point, not a mandated barrier distance. They do not account for ground slope, nearby structures, public access, or overhead services.
  • The app does not assess ground-bearing pressure, rigging design, or crane condition.
  • Lifting operations must be planned and controlled in line with ADOSH-SF CoP 34.0 and any other applicable local regulations, which take precedence over anything shown here.
