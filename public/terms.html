1. What this app is

Duck HSE Portal ("the App") is a planning and record-keeping aid for health, safety and environment (HSE) work: projects and tracked actions, permits to work, equipment inspections, HSE audit evidence, and crane lift assessments. It is provided to help HSE professionals, supervisors and site teams plan, record and track that work. It is not a substitute for:

  • the judgement and sign-off of competent, appropriately qualified people;
  • a site-specific risk assessment and method statement (RAMS);
  • for permits to work, your site's own permit-to-work system and its authorised issuers, and gas testing, energy isolation and rescue arrangements carried out by competent people. The precautions, gas-test limits and close-out checks in the App's permit forms are general examples; your site's procedures and the applicable codes (including ADOSH-SF CoP 21.0 – Permit to Work Systems) take precedence;
  • for lifting operations, the crane manufacturer's certified, current load chart and a competent Appointed Person's independent engineering judgement and sign-off;
  • applicable laws and codes of practice, including the ADOSH-SF codes of practice (for lifting, ADOSH-SF CoP 34.0 – Safe Use of Lifting Equipment and Lifting Accessories) or the equivalent OSH framework in your emirate/free zone, and any Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) occupational safety requirements that apply to your workplace.
