HSE audit preparation

ADOSH-SF Technical Guideline 15 · TAQA WS SOPs
Contractor

Gather implementation evidence: your OSHMS against every ADOSH-SF element, and proof that each TAQA WS SOP is being executed on site — permits, inspections, certificates, training.

Consultant

Gather supervision evidence: your own OSHMS, plus proof you reviewed, approved and monitored the contractor — the duty every TAQA WS SOP assigns to the consultant.

Each checklist point has a serial number and its own upload. Attach the documents as you find them, mark each point's status, then compile the whole pack into one .zip — one folder per S/No., an index page linking every file, and a CSV for Excel — and upload it to Project Number / HSE Audit on Google Drive. Evidence is kept on this device until you do.
