Anyone can read this page and ask for an account to be deleted. To erase data from this device you must be signed in and confirm your password, so nobody else using the device can wipe your records.
What data exists, and where
Your account: email address, free-trial and subscription statusYour account (Google Firebase)
Projects you create or join, their tracked items and activity logYour account (Google Firebase), shared with that project's team
Inspection checklists, permits, lift assessments, signatures, names and notesThis device (browser storage), with a copy in your account while you are signed in
Files and photos added to a project, and that project's backupsThe operator's Google Drive (a shared drive), shared with that project's team through the app
Certificate photos and HSE audit evidence filesThis device only (browser IndexedDB), unless you add them to a project or upload an audit pack to Google Drive
HSE audit packs you upload with Upload to DriveYour own Google Drive, under your Google account. They go straight from your device to your Drive; the operator never receives them, and the Google access token is kept in the page's memory only. They stay under your control: delete them in Google Drive.
Payment detailsHeld by Stripe (website) or Google Play (Android app), never by this app. The app keeps only your plan and its status.
Terms / Privacy acceptance record (which version you accepted, and when)Your account (Google Firebase), and this device
Option 1 — Erase everything on this device

This permanently deletes every checklist, permit, assessment, signature, note, photo, saved preference, and the offline copy of the app from this device, then signs you out. It also removes records that other people who signed in on this device left here. It cannot be undone. Export a backup first from Settings & backup if you need the records. Copies kept in your account are not touched; to delete those, use option 3.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Duck HSE Portal → Storage → Clear storage, deletes all data held on the device as well. In a browser, clear site data for this site from the browser's privacy settings. Neither deletes your account.

Option 3 — Delete your account and everything stored with it

To delete your account and every record kept in it, email the address below from the address the account is registered to, with the subject "Delete my account and data". The operator checks the request and then deletes the account with an admin tool. What that removes is listed below.

Email—
Phone—
What is deletedYour sign-in, your account record (including your Terms and Privacy acceptance record) and the checklists, permits and assessments copied to your account. You are taken off every project: your email is removed from its member list, its invitations and its activity log. Projects you own are handed to another manager or deleted, as you choose. Files you added to a project stay with that project for its team, unless you ask for them to be removed.
Project backupsYour email may remain in a project's existing backups. Backups are kept by number, not by date: the last 30 nightly backups (taken only on days the project changed), up to 20 made by hand and up to 10 made just before a restore. In a project that rarely changes this can be many months. On request, the operator deletes those backups sooner.
Not affectedAudit packs you uploaded to your own Google Drive. They stay in your Drive, under your control; delete them there if you no longer need them.
What may be retained, and whyRecords the operator is required to keep by law (for example accounting/VAT records of a paid subscription), retained only for the statutory period and then deleted
Response timeAcknowledged within 7 days; completed within 30 days

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first, or it will keep renewing: on the website from Manage plan on your dashboard, or in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
