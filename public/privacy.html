Not legal advice. This is a draft starting point, written with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data ("PDPL") in mind. Have it reviewed by a UAE-licensed lawyer before commercial launch — the right approach can differ depending on whether your business is onshore, or licensed in a free zone such as DIFC or ADGM (which have their own, separate data protection regimes that can apply instead of or alongside the federal PDPL).
1. What personal data this app handles

Depending on how you use it, the App may store:

  • Names of assessors, issuers, approvers, verifiers, persons in charge, and contractors entered into assessments and permits, and of the people a permit names for its work, such as fire watchers, confined space entrants, attendants and rescue teams, gas testers, and the people who isolate equipment or close a permit;
  • Permit records such as gas-test readings, isolation points and lock numbers, and close-out notes;
  • Names of inspectors, operators and supervisors, and the equipment identifiers (asset, plate and serial numbers) entered into monthly equipment checklists;
  • Photographs of third-party certificates you attach to a checklist — inspection certificates, operator/rigger/banksman competency certificates and lifting-accessory certificates. These images can contain personal data (a named certificate holder, a photograph on a licence, an issuer's stamp). They are resized and stored on your own device in the browser's IndexedDB, alongside the certificate details (number, issuer, issue and expiry dates) held in the checklist record;
  • Email addresses you type into the checklist forwarding screen. These are used only to address that one message and are handed straight to your own email app — they are not stored by the App, and there is no address book or sending history;
  • Handwritten signatures captured on-screen for permit sign-off;
  • Free-text notes you choose to enter;
  • Your account email address. Your password is held by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to the operator;
  • Project details you enter, the actions, inspections, incidents and other items tracked on a project, the project's activity log, and the email addresses of the people you invite to a project;
  • Files and photos you add to a project (for example method statements, drawings, certificates and site photos), with any note, who added each one and when. These can contain personal data, such as names on a certificate or people in a photo. Photos larger than 2 MB are made smaller on your device before they are sent, which also removes any location data stored inside the photo;
  • Backups of each project: a copy of its details, tracked items, file list and recent activity log.

It does not collect health data, financial/payment card data (card payments are handled entirely by Stripe on the website, or by Google Play in the Android app, not by this app), precise device location, or advertising identifiers.

2. Where this data is stored
  • Your account (email address, free-trial and subscription status), the projects you create or join, and a copy of your checklists, permits and assessments are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which the operator uses as a data processor. See section 3a for where it is hosted.
  • The same checklists, permits and assessments are also kept in your device's browser storage so the App works without a signal.
  • Project files and photos, and project backups, are stored in Google Drive, in a shared drive the operator controls (Google Workspace, used as a data processor). Only the App's server can reach them; members never get direct access to the Drive.
  • Checklist certificate photos and HSE audit evidence files stay in your device's browser storage (IndexedDB) and are not uploaded, unless you add them to a project as files.
  • Members of a project can see its details, tracked items, files and activity log, and the email addresses of the other members. The project's owner and managers can also download its backups. Nobody else can.
  • The operator can access account and project data only as needed to run the service, for example for support, billing and preventing abuse.
  • Clearing your browser's site data deletes the copy on that device. What is stored in your account stays there until you delete the account (see section 11).
3a. Cross-border transfer

The App uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) and Google Drive (for project files and backups) as data processors. Firebase does not currently offer a UAE data-region, so personal data stored in your account is transferred to and stored outside the UAE — in whichever Google Cloud region the Firestore database is created in, which is chosen once and cannot be changed afterwards.

PDPL Articles 22 and 23 permit such a transfer where the destination has an adequate level of protection recognised by the UAE Data Office, or — failing that — where it is covered by an appropriate contractual safeguard or by your explicit consent after being informed of the risk. The safeguard relied on here is Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, together with the consent recorded through the in-app acceptance screen. Using the App requires an account, so if you do not want your data leaving the UAE, do not create an account.

Before commercial launch, confirm with a UAE-licensed lawyer which Firestore region to choose and whether your specific customers (government or critical-infrastructure clients in particular) are subject to data-localisation requirements stricter than the general PDPL position.

3b. Payments and subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are processed by Stripe when bought on the website, and by Google Play Billing when bought in the Android app. The App never sees, receives, or stores your card number or bank details.

  • Website (Stripe). You enter your payment details on Stripe's own checkout page. Your account email address is shared with Stripe so that receipts reach you. The App's server keeps your Stripe customer and subscription reference, plan, status, renewal date, and whether a cancellation is pending, used solely to unlock paid features and let you manage your plan. Stripe's privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy) governs the payment itself.
  • Android app (Google Play). The App receives a purchase token and subscription status from Google, used solely to unlock paid features. Google's own privacy policy governs the payment itself.
4. Legal basis for processing

Where the App processes personal data (as described above), the basis relied on is your consent, given when you accept these terms via the in-app consent screen, and/or the performance of the HSE planning, inspection, audit and record-keeping functions you are using the App for.

5. Your rights under PDPL

Subject to the exceptions PDPL provides, you have the right to: request access to personal data concerning you; request correction or erasure of it; restrict or object to its processing; request its transfer to another controller (data portability); and withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise most of these rights directly: edit or delete your records in the App, export them from the Settings & backup page, or use the Delete your data page.

6. Signatures

On-screen signatures are stored as images alongside the permit record, in the same way as the permit itself. They are used solely to represent sign-off on a permit-to-work document and are not analyzed, shared, or used for any other purpose by the App.

7. Children

This App is intended for use by adult workplace safety professionals and is not directed at children. It should not knowingly be used to collect personal data from children.

8. Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been processed in a way that is not consistent with PDPL, you may raise this with the developer/operator using the contact details in the app footer, and/or with the UAE Data Office, which administers PDPL, or the relevant free-zone data protection authority (DIFC or ADGM Commissioner of Data Protection) if applicable to the entity operating this App.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated as features change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Retention — how long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it; nothing expires on its own, and the developer has no ability to delete it for you. Data in your account is kept while the account is active and deleted within 30 days of an account-deletion request, except where a record must be retained to meet a legal obligation (for example accounting and VAT records relating to a paid subscription, which UAE tax law requires be kept for a set period). Retained records are kept only for that purpose and deleted at the end of the statutory period.

A deleted project file goes to the Google Drive trash, where it is permanently deleted after 30 days. Each project keeps its 30 most recent nightly backups, 20 backups made by hand and 10 backups made just before a restore; older backups are deleted automatically.

11. How to delete your data

Every deletion route — erasing all data on the device, uninstalling, and requesting deletion of your account and the data kept in it — is set out on the Delete your data page. Anyone can read that page and request deletion without signing in; erasing data from a device needs the account's password. It is also the account-deletion URL submitted to Google Play.

12. Data controller and contact

The data controller is the developer/operator of the App, whose current contact details are shown in the app footer and on the About & legal page. Once the operating business is licensed in the UAE, the licensed entity name, trade licence number, and registered address will be published here and in the Google Play listing, as Google Play's developer-verification rules require.

13. Google Play Data Safety

The answers given in the Play Console Data Safety form must mirror this notice: the data types stored in your account (section 2), that they are transmitted over HTTPS, and that deletion can be requested. Google checks the form against the app's actual behaviour, and a mismatch is a policy violation.
