Adding a crane or correcting a load chart

The cranes above are the ones set up in the portal for lift planning. To have a crane added, or a figure in a load chart corrected, contact support at the address on the About & legal page. Send the crane's make and model and a copy of the manufacturer's current load chart (and jib chart, if it has one). Always check the figures for a lift against the load chart supplied with the crane itself.

Merge adds the records in the file that are not on this device yet. Nothing already here is changed.

Replace removes the records on this device first and puts back the versions in the file, also in your account. Use it to go back to an earlier backup. Records saved in your account that are not in the file are not deleted there, and they come back when this device next syncs.
