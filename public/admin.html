Performance targets

These are house targets used by this dashboard to judge performance. They are not regulatory thresholds and nothing here cites one. Replace them with the figures your project HSE plan actually commits to — the compliance scoring, the findings and the action plan all follow whatever you set.

Action response times

How many days an action gets, by the severity of the finding that raised it.

Local AI analyst

The analyst works with no setup — the built-in engine answers from the parsed data and never needs a network. Optionally point it at a model server running on this machine (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp) for free-form phrasing and longer-form writing. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no cloud fallback.
